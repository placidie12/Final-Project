Who We Are

InternTrack is a comprehensive internship management platform designed to bridge the gap between students seeking real-world experience and organizations looking for fresh talent. We work closely with universities and supervisors to ensure every internship is tracked, evaluated, and rewarding for all parties involved.

For Students

Browse internship opportunities, submit applications, and track your progress all in one place.

For Organizations

Post internship positions, review applications, and manage your interns efficiently.

For Supervisors

Monitor student performance, submit evaluations, and provide feedback with ease.

For Universities

Oversee all internship activities, generate reports, and ensure academic compliance.

Secure & Reliable

Built with security in mind — role-based access, email verification, and data protection.
